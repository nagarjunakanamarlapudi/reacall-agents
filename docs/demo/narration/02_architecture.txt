Here is the high-level architecture. Official recall evidence and a 144-lot academic operations dataset enter a bounded agentic RAG layer. Retrieval combines sparse and dense candidates, reciprocal-rank fusion, reranking, a coverage critic, query rewrite, citations, and hard read budgets. An OpenAI model first writes the observable four-role plan. A Deep Agents supervisor then delegates sequentially to regulatory intake, product and lot matching, traceability reconciliation, and containment communications. Each specialist receives typed context and a sealed read-tool surface. Agents can reason and draft, but they cannot write operations. After all required work, an independent source verifier re-reads the evidence and rejects unsupported claims. Only verified evidence may reach the two human gates. Only an approved LangGraph node can call Operations MCP, with version binding, an approval digest, an idempotency key, and a receipt. LangGraph owns durable state, checkpoints, interruption, resume, and closure. This creates a clear authority boundary: the model proposes; evidence, policy, and people decide.
